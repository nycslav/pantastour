# PantasTour
A personalized guide in planning and travelling to the Philippines.

## MVP Features:
- Personalized Discovery & Bucket Lists
    - Suggests nearby establishments, activities, and tourist spots dynamically tailored to the user's current location and stated travel preferences.
    - Allows users to curate personalized bucket lists of dream destinations across the archipelago.

- Interactive Tracking & Localized Gamification
    - Acts as a digital travel journal by logging and mapping the exact locations and destinations the user has visited.
    - Rewards users with culturally relevant titles (e.g., "Island Hopper," "Lechon Connoisseur") upon physically checking in at landmarks, turning exploration into an engaging game.

- Deep-Dive Cultural & Historical Guides
    - Delivers concise historical context and descriptive backgrounds for each destination.
    - Provides granular cultural etiquette instructions, moving beyond basic "do's and don'ts" to cover practical daily interactions like tipping culture, pasalubong (souvenir) traditions, and kamayan (eating with hands).

- Real-Time Safety & Weather Alerts
    - Integrates live PAGASA weather updates directly into the travel planning interface.
    - Pushes critical safety alerts, such as "Safe to Travel" statuses or sudden ferry cancellations due to monsoon conditions, ensuring user safety.

- Festival & Event Tracker
    - Highlights upcoming regional festivals (fiestas) based on the user's location or itinerary.
    - Sends timely notifications and provides survival guides for navigating major events like Sinulog or Ati-Atihan.


## Premium Features:
- Complete Offline Mode: Internet connectivity in remote destinations (e.g., Palawan, Siargao, Batanes) is often unreliable. Gate offline maps, offline survival phrases, and saved itineraries behind the paywall.
- AI-Powered Itinerary Generator: Free users get single-destination recommendations. Premium users input their stay duration, budget, and travel style, and the app generates a day-by-day, highly optimized itinerary spanning multiple islands.
- The "Hidden Gem" Database: Mainstream spots (like Boracay's White Beach) are available to all. Premium users unlock off-the-beaten-path locations, hidden waterfalls, and local-secret carinderias (eateries) to avoid tourist traps.
- Collaborative Group Trips: Traveling in the Philippines is often done in large groups ("barkada"). Premium users can invite friends to a shared workspace to vote on bucket-list items, split expenses, and co-edit itineraries.
- Ad-Free & Priority Booking Notifications: Remove all ads and provide early push notifications when highly anticipated local festivals (like Sinulog or Ati-Atihan) have nearby hotel availabilities dropping.
